# Monopoly Master - Project Rules & Guidelines

## 1. Strictly English-Only Rule

- **CRITICAL MANDATE**: ALL in-game text, UI labels, buttons, modals, toasts, tooltips, dialogs, game logs, commentary, and user-facing notifications MUST be in **ENGLISH ONLY**.
- **NEVER** use Uzbek, Russian, or any language other than English anywhere in the game interface, game engine, or codebase.
- The entire application is English-only.

## 2. Audio & Visual Synchronization

- Maintain precise duration alignment between sound effects and their corresponding visual animations and toasts.
